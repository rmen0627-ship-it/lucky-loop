Good to know.

  • Birthday parties

    Ten players. One shady table.

    Up to 10 players, 18 holes each, raspas for everyone and a reserved shade table for 90 minutes.

    $180party ticket

    Message us (opens Instagram)

  • The Snack Shack

    Raspas, lemonade, popcorn.

    A raspa is Houston's snow cone: shaved ice and syrup, eaten fast.

  • Accessibility

    Every hole, every player.

    All 18 holes are wheelchair accessible, with paved paths, level tees, shade sails over every green, misting fans on the back nine and free parking.

  • Rain policy

    Lightning stops play.

    If lightning closes the course, everyone on it gets a rain check for another round.

Official rules

The Wall is a monthly drawing. Here is every rule.

The basics

OFFICIAL RULES (SHORT VERSION): NO PURCHASE NECESSARY. A purchase does not improve your chances of winning. Open to legal U.S. residents 18 and older. Kids may appear in clips, but a parent or guardian must post the entry.

How to enter

To enter, post a video of a hole-in-one at Lucky Loop with #TeeOffAtLuckyLoop and tag @luckyloopfbla during the month. To enter without playing, send us a direct message with your name and "Wall entry" (one free entry per person per month).

The drawing

Each month, Lucky Loop staff draw one winner at random from all eligible entries within 5 days after the month ends and notify the winner by direct message. The winner must reply within 7 days or an alternate is drawn.

Prize and odds

Prize: one family 4-pack (four rounds of mini golf, approximate retail value $40). Odds depend on the number of entries. Void where prohibited.
